Legal Architecture

Every Song Has Two Copyrights

Understanding the legal split between the Master Sound Recording and the Composition Composition Copyright.

Sound Recording Copyright

1. The Master Right (℗)

The Master copyright covers the specific audio recording rendered into WAV or FLAC files. Controlled by recording artists, producers, or record labels.

Primary Payout Channels: Spotify, Apple, Digital Retailers
Collection Agent: Digital Music Distributor (Audiory)
Code Identifier: ISRC Code
Composition Copyright

2. The Composition Right (©)

The Composition represents the underlying musical composition: sheet music, melody, chord progressions, and lyrics. Controlled by Songwriters and Music Publishers.

Primary Payout Channels: Performance Rights Organizations (PROs)
Collection Agent: BMI, ASCAP, PRS, Mechanical Licensing Collective
Code Identifier: ISWC Code

Revenue Streams Explored

The 4 Core Music Royalty Streams

01

Master Streaming Royalties

Generated whenever an audio master file is streamed on interactive platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music. Paid directly to distributor.

Collected by: Audiory
02

Digital Mechanical Royalties

Triggered when music is reproduced digitally (e.g., streaming playback or direct downloads). Covers composition reproduction rights.

Collected by: MLC / Publishers
03

Performance Royalties

Generated when music is performed publicly, broadcasted on terrestrial radio, TV shows, venue speakers, or digital streams.

Collected by: PROs (ASCAP/BMI)
04

Synchronization (Sync) Fees

Upfront licensing fees generated when audio is synchronized with visual media like movies, video games, commercials, or TV series.

Collected by: Sync Licensing Agents

Music Industry Terms

Essential Royalty Glossary

Identification Code

ISRC Code

International Standard Recording Code. A unique 12-character alphanumeric identifier assigned to a specific audio recording master.

Publishing Code

ISWC Code

International Standard Musical Work Code. Unique identifier for musical compositions (lyrics and melodies) distinct from recording masters.

Legal Concept

Recoupment

The process where a record label or distributor retains incoming royalties to repay an upfront financial advance or production expense.

Organization

PRO (Performance Rights Org)

Agencies like BMI, ASCAP, and PRS that collect public performance royalties on behalf of songwriters and publishers.

Rights Admin

The MLC

The Mechanical Licensing Collective. U.S. organization established to collect digital mechanical royalties from interactive DSPs.

Fingerprinting

YouTube Content ID

An automated digital fingerprinting system that scans user-uploaded videos for matching audio and monetizes them for copyright holders.

Legal Workflow

How Infringement Claims Are Handled

01

Notice Submitted

Copyright owner files a formal, signed DMCA notification identifying the infringing master WAV/metadata or ISRC track code.

02

Takedown & Lock

Audiory promptly removes or disables access to the targeted track across DSP platforms and freezes associated pending royalties.

03

Uploader Notified

The account owner is notified immediately with a copy of the claim and given 10–14 business days to submit a legal Counter-Notice.

04

Resolution / Court Action

If no court lawsuit is filed within 14 days following counter-notice, the content and frozen royalties are restored to the uploader.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device by web applications you visit. In addition to cookies, Audiory utilizes browser HTML5 Local Storage, Session Storage, and Pixel Tags (web beacons) to store authentication state, optimize music audio playback performance, and collect aggregate usage data.

2. How We Use Cookies on Audiory Platforms

Audiory uses tracking technologies across two distinct areas of our ecosystem:

Creator Dashboard & App

Maintains secure login sessions, preserves your split calculation tables, remembers active upload workflows, and prevents cross-site request forgery (CSRF).

Smart Links & Landing Pages

Measures fan click-through rates (CTR) to Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube. Tracks geographic source and referrer domains without storing PII.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Ad Pixels

Audiory allows record labels and independent artists to optionally integrate third-party tracking scripts into their custom Smart Links (e.g., Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Ads). When fans interact with these links, third-party cookies may be set by those platforms subject to their respective privacy policies.

4. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can restrict or block cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact dashboard authentication, catalog upload features, or split payout functions.
