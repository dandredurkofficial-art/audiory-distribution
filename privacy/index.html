Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 5, 2026 — Applicable to https://distro.audiory.site/

1. Information We Collect

When you use our portal at distro.audiory.site, we collect personal and music metadata essential for delivering your catalog to streaming services and processing royalty payouts:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, password hash, and profile details provided via manual signup or Google Sign-In.
  • Music Metadata: Track titles, ISRC/UPC codes, songwriter/composer credits, cover art files, and audio assets.
  • Financial & Payout Data: Payment details (PayPal email, direct deposit bank info) and tax declaration forms required for processing royalty payouts.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, and session logs logged automatically for fraud prevention.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is strictly processed for legitimate music distribution operations:

  • Delivering and encoding your music for Digital Service Providers (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, etc.).
  • Calculating, processing, and remitting monthly royalty earnings to your account balance.
  • Verifying account ownership, preventing copyright infringements, and curbing artificial streaming behavior.
  • Sending system updates, payout notices, and technical security alerts.

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. We share necessary data only with authorized service partners required to operate our service:

DSPs & Streaming Partners: Track metadata and audio files are transmitted directly to store endpoints (Spotify, Apple, YouTube, etc.).

Payment Processors: Payout details are routed securely through compliant financial networks (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, ACH banks).

Firebase Infrastructure: Authentication data and user records are hosted securely within Google Cloud Platform infrastructure.

4. Data Security & Storage

We enforce strict security safeguards to protect your personal information and media files. All transactions across distro.audiory.site are encrypted using HTTPS (TLS 1.3). Database rules restrict unauthorized access so that users can only read or modify their own catalog and payout accounts.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your region (including GDPR and CCPA regulations), you possess rights regarding your data:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data and release metadata we hold.
  • Correction: Modify outdated profile or payout details directly through your dashboard.
  • Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request full deletion of your user profile and catalog takedown (subject to royalty audit retention requirements).

6. Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have questions, data deletion requests, or concerns regarding your privacy at distro.audiory.site, please reach out to us:
